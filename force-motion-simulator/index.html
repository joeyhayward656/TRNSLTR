How It Works

This simulator uses Newton's Second Law: the net force on an object equals its mass times its acceleration (F = ma).

Net Force

Fnet,x = Fright − Fleft

Fnet,y = Fdown − Fup

Acceleration

ax = Fnet,x / m

ay = Fnet,y / m

Velocity & Position

v = u + at

s = ut + ½at²

Speed & Direction

speed = √(vx² + vy²)

direction = atan2(vy, vx)

When the net force is zero, acceleration is zero and the object moves at constant velocity (Newton's First Law). Opposing forces of equal magnitude cancel each other out.
